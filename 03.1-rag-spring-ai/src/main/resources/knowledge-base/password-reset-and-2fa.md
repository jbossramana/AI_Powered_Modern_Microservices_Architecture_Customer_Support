# Resetting Your Password and Managing Two-Factor Authentication

## Resetting a forgotten password

1. Go to the sign-in page and click "Forgot password?"
2. Enter the email address on your account. We send a reset link that is valid for
   30 minutes.
3. Click the link and choose a new password. Passwords must be at least 10 characters
   and include a number and a symbol.
4. You'll be signed out of all devices except the one you used to reset the password.
   Sign in again on your other devices with the new password.

If you don't receive the reset email within a few minutes, check your spam folder, then
confirm you're entering the exact email address the account was created with. Free-plan
accounts can request at most 3 password resets per 24 hours; Pro and Business accounts
have no such limit.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

2FA is optional on the Free and Pro plans and **mandatory** for all members of a
Business-plan workspace. To enable it: Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication →
choose an authenticator app (we support any TOTP app - Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password,
etc.) or SMS.

If you lose access to your 2FA device, use one of the 10 backup codes generated when you
first enabled 2FA. If you don't have your backup codes, account recovery requires
verifying your identity with our support team - this can take up to 2 business days for
security reasons and cannot be expedited over chat.

## Account lockouts

After 5 failed sign-in attempts, an account is locked for 15 minutes. This is a security
measure and cannot be manually cleared by support before the timer expires.
