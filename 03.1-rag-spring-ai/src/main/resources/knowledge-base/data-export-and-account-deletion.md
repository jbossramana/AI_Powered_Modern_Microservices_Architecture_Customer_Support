# Exporting Your Data and Deleting Your Account

## Exporting your data

Settings → Privacy → Export My Data. We generate a downloadable ZIP of all files you own
(not files shared with you by others) plus your account metadata (sharing history,
account activity log). Exports for accounts under 50 GB are usually ready within 15
minutes; larger accounts can take several hours. You'll get an email with a download
link valid for 7 days.

## Deleting your account

Settings → Privacy → Delete Account. This requires re-entering your password and is
**not reversible after 30 days**. What happens:

- **Days 0–30**: the account is deactivated but recoverable. Signing in during this
  window offers to restore the account exactly as it was.
- **After 30 days**: all files, sharing history, and account metadata are permanently
  deleted from production systems. Encrypted backups are purged within a further 90 days
  per our data retention policy.

Deleting your account cancels any active subscription immediately; you are not charged
again, and per the refund policy, prior charges for the current period are not
automatically refunded unless you're within the standard 14-day refund window.

## What happens to files you shared with others

Files you own that are shared with others become inaccessible to them once your account
is permanently deleted (after the 30-day window). If a collaborator needs to keep a
shared file, they should make their own copy before the 30-day window ends - support
cannot restore access to a deleted account's files afterward.

## Business workspace accounts

An individual member leaving a Business workspace does not delete their personal
account, only their workspace membership. Deleting an entire Business workspace requires
workspace-owner permissions and follows the same 30-day recovery window, applied to the
whole workspace at once.
