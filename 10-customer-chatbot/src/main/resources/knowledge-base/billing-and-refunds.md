# Billing, Invoices, and Refunds

## Billing cycle

Pro and Business plans are billed monthly on the calendar day you first subscribed, or
annually if you chose an annual plan at checkout (annual plans get a 15% discount over
monthly). Charges are attempted at 09:00 UTC on the billing date. If a charge fails, we
retry after 3 days and again after 7 days before downgrading the account to Free.

## Refund policy

- **Within 14 days of first subscribing**: full refund, no questions asked. Contact
  support or use Settings → Billing → Request Refund.
- **Annual plans, after 14 days**: refunded on a prorated basis for unused months, minus
  a 5% processing fee, at support's discretion.
- **Monthly plans, after 14 days**: no refunds for the current billing period, but you
  can cancel to stop future charges. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the current
  paid period; you keep plan features until then.
- **Duplicate charges or billing errors**: always fully refunded, no time limit. If a
  customer reports being charged twice or charged the wrong amount, this should be
  escalated to a human agent to verify against the payment processor records rather than
  resolved from chat alone.

## Downgrading or cancelling

Settings → Billing → Change Plan. Downgrading from Business or Pro to Free is immediate
for feature access, but if the account is storing more data than the Free plan's 5 GB
limit, the account becomes **read-only** (no new uploads) until it's back under the
limit or the customer upgrades again. Files are never deleted for being over a storage
limit.

## Seats (Business plan only)

Business plans are billed per seat, per month or year. Adding a seat mid-cycle is
prorated for the remainder of the current period. Removing a seat takes effect at the
next billing date, not immediately - removed members retain access until then.
