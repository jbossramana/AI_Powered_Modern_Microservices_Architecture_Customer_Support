# Storage Limits and Upgrading Plans

## Current plan limits

| Plan | Storage | Max file size | Devices |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 5 GB | 2 GB | 1 |
| Pro | 2 TB | 50 GB | 5 |
| Business | 10 TB per seat, pooled across the workspace | 100 GB | Unlimited |

## Upgrading

Settings → Billing → Change Plan → select the new plan. Upgrades take effect
immediately - the new storage limit and features are available as soon as payment is
confirmed, usually within a few seconds. You're charged a prorated amount for the rest
of the current billing period, then the full price from the next cycle onward.

## What happens when you hit your storage limit

Accounts over their storage limit become **read-only**: existing files stay safe and
accessible for viewing/downloading and sharing, but new uploads and edits are blocked
until the account is back under the limit (by deleting files or upgrading). We never
delete a customer's files for being over the limit.

## Increasing storage without upgrading plan tier

Pro customers can purchase storage add-ons in 500 GB increments without moving to
Business. Business customers can add seats (each seat includes 10 TB, pooled) rather
than purchasing storage separately. There is no way to add storage to a Free account
without upgrading to Pro or Business - Free's 5 GB limit is fixed.

## Checking current usage

Settings → Storage shows a breakdown by file type and by shared vs. owned files. The
breakdown can take up to 24 hours to reflect very recent large deletions, even though the
overall storage number updates within minutes.
